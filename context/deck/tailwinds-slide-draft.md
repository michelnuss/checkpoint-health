# Tailwinds slide (drafted, not yet added to the deck)

Headline: "Six tailwinds, all blowing the same way."
Layout idea: 3 x 2 grid, one tailwind per cell: big number, one line, small source. Could replace slides 2 and 3, or sit as slide 4 (deck stays at 12 max).

1. **MCP is now the standard way AI connects to tools.** 97 million monthly SDK downloads (Mar 25, 2026). Anthropic donated MCP to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation in Dec 2025 (co-founded with Block and OpenAI). Build once, works in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot.
2. **AI is becoming the front door to health.** More than 300 million people ask ChatGPT health questions every week (OpenAI, Jul 2026). Health in ChatGPT launched to US users in July 2026 with medical-record connections. The AI companies are building intake; none of them supplies a doctor.
3. **On-demand care.** 18.4% of commercially insured patients had a telehealth claim in Q1 2026 (FAIR Health). Hims & Hers paid up to $1.15B for Eucalyptus (Feb 2026).
4. **Telemedicine keeps growing, and Florida allows it.** Telehealth claim lines up 10.1% quarter over quarter (FAIR Health Q1 2026). Florida lets out-of-state clinicians register for telehealth (Fla. Stat. 456.47).
5. **Doctors are hard to reach.** Average wait for a new patient appointment is 31 days, up 19% since 2022 (AMN Healthcare, 2025). AAMC projects a shortage of 20,200 to 40,400 primary care physicians by 2036.
6. **Doctors already work with AI.** 81% of physicians use AI in their practice, up from 38% in 2023 (AMA, 2026). "Clinician edits the AI's draft" is a normal workflow.

Optional seventh: patients pay more out of pocket. More than a third of covered workers have a deductible of $2,000 or more, up 32% in five years (KFF 2025).

Watch out: AMN found Miami family medicine waits average only 4 days. Say "nationally, 31 days" and lean on the late-night angle.
